# Motivation Letter — OAS Academic Scholarships Program

**Organization:** OAS / OEA
**URL:** https://www.oas.org/en/scholarships/
**Amount:** Matrícula + manutención + seguro
**Deadline:** Ver oas.org (usualmente Feb-Mar y Jul-Aug)

---

The vulnerability of IoT and embedded systems to cyber-attacks is a critical bottleneck for the digital transformation of Latin American infrastructure. Current cryptographic standards often exceed the computational constraints of low-power devices, creating a gap between security and efficiency. My research addresses this by developing lightweight, high-performance hashing algorithms capable of securing edge computing without sacrificing speed.

My development of FCH-ARX V4 serves as a proof of concept for this ambition. By achieving a 49.95% avalanche effect and $2^{256}$ collision resistance—passing NIST FIPS 180-4 validation—I have demonstrated that original, publishable security architectures can be engineered to optimize data integrity. However, pursuing this research independently while working full-time as a Commercial Advisor has reached a ceiling. While my professional role provides stability, it creates a structural gap between my capacity and my academic potential. This scholarship is the essential catalyst that would allow me to transition from fragmented independent study to full-time, rigorous research.

My unique strength lies in my dual academic foundation. Combining an Economics degree from UTM with Data Science and AI Engineering from the University of Guayaquil allows me to approach AI not just as a technical tool, but as an economic optimizer. I view the integration of Machine Learning and Cryptography through the lens of resource allocation and systemic efficiency, a perspective that is rare in traditional Computer Science profiles.

With the support of the OAS, my objective is to pursue a Master’s/PhD focusing on the intersection of AI-driven cryptography and secure embedded systems. My plan is to evolve FCH-ARX into a suite of adaptive security protocols that use ML to dynamically adjust encryption strength based on real-time threat levels. By bridging the gap between theoretical mathematics and applied AI, I aim to contribute to the technological sovereignty and cybersecurity resilience of the OAS member states.
