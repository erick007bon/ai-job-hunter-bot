---

The security of IoT and embedded systems is currently hindered by a critical trade-off: the need for robust cryptographic security versus the limited computational resources of edge devices. My research addresses this gap through the development of FCH-ARX V4, a cryptographic hash algorithm specifically designed for high-performance environments. By achieving a 49.95% avalanche effect and $2^{256}$ collision resistance—meeting NIST FIPS 180-4 validation standards—I have demonstrated that it is possible to implement rigorous security without compromising hardware efficiency.

However, scaling this innovation requires a transition from independent experimentation to formal academic research. Currently, I balance my intellectual pursuits with a role as a Commercial Advisor at Vamoret S.A. While this experience has honed my professionalism, it creates a systemic gap between my capacity for discovery and the time required for rigorous scientific validation. The OAS Academic Scholarship would provide the necessary catalyst to move beyond self-directed study and commit entirely to a graduate program specializing in AI and Cybersecurity.

My unique strength lies in my dual academic foundation in Economics and Data Science & AI Engineering. This interdisciplinary perspective allows me to approach AI not just as a technical tool, but as an optimization problem, applying econometric causal inference and time-series analysis to evaluate the stability and efficiency of AI-driven cryptographic models.

With this funding, my research plan is to integrate my hash algorithm into a multi-agent AI framework using Model Context Protocol (MCP). I intend to develop a self-optimizing security layer for IoT networks where LLM-driven agents can autonomously detect vulnerabilities and rotate cryptographic keys in real-time. By bridging the gap between theoretical cryptography and applied AI, I aim to contribute a scalable, secure infrastructure that empowers Latin American technological sovereignty, ensuring our regional digital transformation is built on a foundation of sovereign and resilient security.
