# Motivation Letter — OAS Academic Scholarships Program

**Organization:** OAS / OEA
**URL:** https://www.oas.org/en/scholarships/
**Amount:** Matrícula + manutención + seguro
**Deadline:** Ver oas.org (usualmente Feb-Mar y Jul-Aug)

---

The current vulnerability of IoT and embedded systems stems from a critical trade-off: the need for high-level security versus the limited computational resources of edge devices. Traditional cryptographic standards are often too heavy for these environments, creating a security gap that threatens critical infrastructure. My research addresses this by developing lightweight, collision-resistant hashing algorithms that ensure data integrity without compromising system performance.

My development of the FCH-ARX V4 algorithm represents a tangible contribution to this field. By achieving a 49.95% avalanche effect and 2^256 collision resistance, FCH-ARX V4 successfully passed NIST FIPS 180-4 validation. However, producing this result independently while working full-time as a Commercial Advisor has highlighted a significant gap: the lack of institutional infrastructure and dedicated research time necessary to scale these findings into publishable, peer-reviewed contributions. This scholarship would allow me to transition from self-taught experimentation to rigorous academic research.

My profile offers a unique intersection of disciplines. Combining an Economics degree with ongoing studies in Data Science and AI Engineering allows me to approach cryptography not just as a mathematical challenge, but as an optimization problem. I possess the econometric rigor for causal inference and time-series analysis, paired with the technical capacity to implement MLOps and multi-agent LLM architectures. This dual perspective is essential for designing AI-driven security protocols that are both mathematically sound and economically viable.

With the OAS scholarship, I intend to pursue a Master’s degree focusing on the integration of AI-optimized cryptography for secure edge computing. My specific plan is to evolve FCH-ARX V4 into a dynamic framework where machine learning models optimize hashing parameters in real-time based on the device's energy constraints. By bridging the gap between theoretical cryptography and applied AI, I aim to develop scalable security solutions that strengthen the digital sovereignty and technological resilience of the Latin American region.
