The security of IoT and embedded systems is currently threatened by the inefficiency of traditional cryptographic standards, which often exceed the computational limits of low-power devices. My research addresses this vulnerability through the development of FCH-ARX V4, an original cryptographic hash algorithm. By achieving a 49.95% avalanche effect and $2^{256}$ collision resistance, FCH-ARX V4 successfully passed NIST FIPS 180-4 validation, proving that high-security standards can be implemented without compromising hardware efficiency.

My academic profile is built on a unique duality: a degree in Economics from UTM and an ongoing specialization in Data Science and AI Engineering at the University of Guayaquil. This combination allows me to approach AI research not just as a technical challenge, but as an optimization problem, integrating econometrics (causal inference and time-series analysis) with advanced MLOps and LLM agent architectures. This interdisciplinary perspective is essential for creating secure, scalable AI systems that are mathematically robust and economically viable.

However, a significant gap exists between my research potential and my current circumstances. While I have developed FCH-ARX V4 independently, I currently serve as a Commercial Advisor at Vamoret S.A. While this role has honed my professional discipline, it is disconnected from my scientific aspirations. The OAS Scholarship is the critical catalyst I need to transition from independent study to a rigorous postgraduate environment, allowing me to dedicate my full cognitive capacity to high-impact research.

With this funding, I intend to pursue a Master’s/PhD focusing on the integration of lightweight cryptography and AI to secure edge computing nodes. My goal is to evolve FCH-ARX V4 into a framework for secure, decentralized AI agents, ensuring data integrity in critical Latin American infrastructure. By bridging the gap between theoretical cryptography and applied AI, I aim to contribute to the region's technological sovereignty and digital security.
