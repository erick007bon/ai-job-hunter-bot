This scholarship would bridge that gap, allowing me to transition from an independent researcher to a full-time AI security scholar.

My goal is to publish these findings in top-tier venues (such as NeurIPS or USENIX) and open-source a library that allows developers to implement lightweight security in embedded AI. This funding is the catalyst required to transform my mathematical prototypes into scalable, industry-standard security protocols.
