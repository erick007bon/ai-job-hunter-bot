By achieving a 49.95% avalanche effect and $2^{256}$ collision resistance, FCH-ARX V4 has already passed NIST FIPS 180-4 validation, proving its viability as a secure foundation for data integrity in constrained environments.

My academic trajectory is defined by a dual perspective: the analytical rigor of Economics (UTM) combined with the technical depth of Data Science and AI Engineering (U. Guayaquil). This interdisciplinary background allows me to approach security not just as a coding challenge, but as an optimization problem where computational cost and security margins must be balanced—a perspective directly aligned with Google Research’s goals in Security, Privacy, and Abuse Prevention.

Despite these achievements, my current professional role as a Commercial Advisor at Vamoret S.A. exists outside the AI domain. This scholarship would bridge the gap between my current employment and my potential as a full-time researcher, providing the dedicated focus required to transition into high-impact AI security.

With this funding, I plan to integrate my hash algorithm with Machine Learning models to develop "AI-driven anomaly detection for encrypted traffic," specifically focusing on identifying adversarial patterns in IoT networks without decrypting sensitive data. By leveraging my expertise in causal inference and multi-agent systems, I aim to publish a framework that enhances the resilience of embedded systems against quantum-era threats, contributing a scalable, open-source solution to the global research community.
