The critical vulnerability of IoT and embedded systems in developing regions lies in the trade-off between cryptographic security and computational overhead. Current standards often exceed the processing capabilities of low-power devices, leaving infrastructure exposed. My research focuses on solving this through the development of lightweight, high-performance hashing algorithms that ensure data integrity without compromising hardware efficiency.

My development of the FCH-ARX V4 algorithm demonstrates this commitment. By achieving a 49.95% avalanche effect and $2^{256}$ collision resistance, the model successfully passed NIST FIPS 180-4 validation. This achievement proves my ability to conduct original, publishable research at the intersection of mathematics and computer science. However, conducting this research independently while working full-time as a Commercial Advisor at Vamoret S.A. has highlighted a significant gap: I possess the technical capacity, but lack the structured academic environment and advanced computational resources necessary to scale these innovations.

My double academic background in Economics (UTM) and Data Science & AI (U. Guayaquil) provides a unique interdisciplinary lens. While my economics training allows me to analyze the cost-benefit ratios and systemic impacts of technology deployment, my engineering training enables the technical implementation of LLMs and MLOps. This duality allows me to approach AI not just as a tool, but as a scalable solution for regional development.

With the DAAD scholarship, I intend to transition from independent study to full-time research in Germany, focusing on integrating my FCH-ARX framework with AI-driven anomaly detection for secure embedded systems. My goal is to develop a self-healing security layer for industrial IoT that can be deployed in Latin American infrastructure. By merging German engineering excellence with my background in quantitative analysis, I aim to create secure, automated systems that foster sustainable technological sovereignty in Ecuador and beyond.
