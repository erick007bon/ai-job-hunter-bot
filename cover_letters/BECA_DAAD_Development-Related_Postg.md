The rapid proliferation of IoT and embedded systems in Latin America has exposed a critical vulnerability: the trade-off between computational efficiency and cryptographic security. Standard encryption often exceeds the hardware constraints of low-power devices, creating security gaps in critical infrastructure. My research focuses on bridging this gap through the development of lightweight, high-performance cryptographic primitives optimized for these environments.

My primary contribution is the development of FCH-ARX V4, a cryptographic hash algorithm that successfully passed NIST FIPS 180-4 validation, achieving a 49.95% avalanche effect and $2^{256}$ collision resistance. This original work demonstrates my ability to synthesize complex mathematical rigor with practical implementation. However, pursuing this research independently while working full-time as a Commercial Advisor has limited my capacity to scale these findings. The DAAD scholarship is the essential catalyst I need to transition from independent experimentation to rigorous, full-time academic research.

My dual background in Economics (UTM) and Data Science & AI (U. Guayaquil) provides a unique multidisciplinary perspective. I combine the analytical rigor of econometrics and causal inference with the technical capacity of MLOps and LLM architectures. This allows me to not only build secure systems but to evaluate their socio-economic viability and scalability in developing markets.

With this funding, my objective is to specialize in the intersection of AI and Cryptography in Germany. My research plan involves integrating machine learning to optimize the internal permutations of the FCH-ARX algorithm, reducing latency in resource-constrained IoT devices without sacrificing entropy. By mastering advanced STEM methodologies in Germany, I intend to return to Ecuador to implement secure, AI-driven frameworks that protect national digital infrastructure. I am eager to leverage Germany's technological leadership to transform my theoretical breakthroughs into scalable solutions that drive sustainable digital development in Latin America.
