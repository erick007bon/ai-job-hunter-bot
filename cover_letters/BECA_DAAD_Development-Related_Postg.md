The increasing vulnerability of IoT and embedded systems in developing regions demands lightweight yet robust cryptographic standards. Current algorithms often impose prohibitive computational overhead on low-power hardware, creating a security gap in critical infrastructure. My research addresses this by optimizing the trade-off between collision resistance and computational efficiency through the development of the FCH-ARX V4 algorithm. This cryptographic hash passed NIST FIPS 180-4 validation, achieving a 49.95% avalanche effect and $2^{256}$ collision resistance, proving that high-security standards can be achieved through optimized ARX (Addition-Rotation-XOR) architectures.

While my current role as a Commercial Advisor at Vamoret S.A. provides professional stability, it creates a significant gap between my capacity and my ambitions. I have developed FCH-ARX V4 and my AI expertise independently, balancing full-time employment with rigorous self-study. This scholarship is the essential catalyst I need to transition from an independent researcher to a full-time specialist, allowing me to dedicate my total focus to advanced AI research.

My unique strength lies in my double academic background. Combining an Economics degree from UTM with a Data Science & AI Engineering track from the University of Guayaquil allows me to approach technical problems through a lens of resource optimization and cost-benefit analysis. I don't just build models; I evaluate their economic feasibility and scalability in real-world deployments.

With DAAD funding, I intend to integrate my cryptographic research with Machine Learning to develop "Adaptive Cryptographic Agents." My plan is to build a framework where LLMs and multi-agent systems (via MCP) optimize encryption parameters in real-time based on the hardware's energy constraints and threat levels. By pursuing a Master’s in Germany, I aim to bridge the gap between theoretical cryptography and applied AI, bringing this high-level technical sovereignty back to Ecuador to strengthen our digital infrastructure.
