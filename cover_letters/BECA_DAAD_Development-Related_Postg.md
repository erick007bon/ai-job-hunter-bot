The critical vulnerability of IoT and embedded systems in developing nations lies in the trade-off between cryptographic security and computational overhead. Current standards often exceed the energy and processing capacities of low-cost hardware, leaving essential infrastructure prone to attacks. My research focuses on bridging this gap by developing lightweight, high-performance cryptographic primitives that ensure data integrity without sacrificing system efficiency.

My development of the FCH-ARX V4 algorithm demonstrates this commitment. By achieving a 49.95% avalanche effect and $2^{256}$ collision resistance—passing NIST FIPS 180-4 validation—I have proven that original, publishable security architectures can be engineered to optimize resource-constrained environments. However, scaling these findings into a robust AI-driven framework requires the advanced academic rigor and technical infrastructure available in Germany.

Currently, I navigate a significant gap: while I possess a double academic foundation in Economics and Data Science & AI Engineering, I perform this research independently while working as a Commercial Advisor. This professional disconnect limits my capacity for full-time experimentation. Transitioning from a commercial role to a dedicated research environment via the DAAD scholarship is the essential catalyst I need to pivot my career toward AI research. My interdisciplinary background allows me to approach AI not just as a coding challenge, but as an economic optimization problem, ensuring that the technological solutions I develop are both scalable and sustainable for Latin American markets.

With this funding, I plan to investigate the integration of Model Context Protocol (MCP) and multi-agent systems to automate the detection of cryptographic vulnerabilities in embedded systems. My objective is to develop a self-healing security layer that utilizes machine learning to adapt encryption parameters in real-time based on threat vectors. By combining German engineering excellence with my multidisciplinary profile, I aim to contribute to the digital sovereignty and cybersecurity resilience of Ecuador.
