The challenge of securing data integrity in resource-constrained environments remains a critical bottleneck for the scaling of decentralized AI and IoT ecosystems. While current cryptographic standards are robust, they often impose computational overheads that hinder real-time deployment in embedded systems. My research focuses on optimizing the intersection of cryptographic hashing and machine learning to create lightweight, secure frameworks for intelligent automation.

My development of the FCH-ARX V4 algorithm represents a significant step toward this goal. By achieving a 49.95% avalanche effect and $2^{256}$ collision resistance—passing NIST FIPS 180-4 validation—I have demonstrated the ability to engineer original, high-performance cryptographic solutions from the ground up. Integrating this with my expertise in LLMs and multi-agent systems via the Model Context Protocol (MCP), I aim to build autonomous agents capable of verifiable, secure computation.

Currently, I balance my research independently while working as a Commercial Advisor at Vamoret S.A. While this professional experience has honed my discipline, the gap between my current role and my research ambitions is vast. This residency would provide the critical leap necessary to transition from independent experimentation to full-time, high-impact research, allowing me to dedicate 100% of my cognitive bandwidth to AI safety and security.

My double background in Economics and Data Science grants me a unique perspective: I view AI not just as a technical tool, but as a system of incentives and resource optimization. I combine econometric rigor (causal inference and time-series analysis) with the technical agility of a machine learning engineer.

At OpenAI, I propose to research the implementation of secure, cryptographic verification layers for LLM-driven agents to prevent prompt injection and data leakage in distributed systems. By leveraging the residency’s resources, I intend to evolve FCH-ARX into a standard for secure, low-latency communication between AI agents, ensuring that the future of AGI is both scalable and cryptographically verifiable.
