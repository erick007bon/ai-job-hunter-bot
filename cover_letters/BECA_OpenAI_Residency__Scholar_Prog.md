Currently, I balance these research ambitions with a full-time role as a Commercial Advisor. While this demonstrates my discipline, the gap between my professional duties and my research potential is significant. I aim to explore how verifiable cryptographic proofs can be embedded into LLM reasoning chains to prevent hallucinations in financial forecasting and automate secure, trustless data exchanges between autonomous agents. 

I am not seeking a traditional academic path, but a residency where my non-traditional profile—blending economics, cryptography, and AI—can contribute to the safety and scalability of AGI. I am ready to transition from the periphery of the field to the epicenter of AI research.
