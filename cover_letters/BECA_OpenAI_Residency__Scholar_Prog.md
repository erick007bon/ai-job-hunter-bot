My research addresses this gap by optimizing hash functions for resource-constrained environments. I developed FCH-ARX V4, a cryptographic hash algorithm that successfully passed NIST FIPS 180-4 validation, demonstrating a 49.95% avalanche effect and $2^{256}$ collision resistance. This work proves that high-security primitives can be engineered without compromising efficiency—a prerequisite for the next generation of edge-deployed AI agents.

By leveraging my background in both cryptography and MLOps, I plan to build secure, privacy-preserving protocols that allow AI agents to operate autonomously in sensitive environments. My goal is to contribute to the safety and reliability of AGI by ensuring that the underlying data pipelines are cryptographically sound and computationally efficient, bridging the gap between theoretical security and scalable AI implementation.
