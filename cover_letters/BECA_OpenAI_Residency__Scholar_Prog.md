My research focuses on this intersection, specifically developing lightweight hashing mechanisms that can withstand collision attacks without compromising computational overhead. My dual background in Economics (UTM) and Data Science & AI Engineering (U. Guayaquil) gives me a unique multidisciplinary lens. I aim to create a framework where LLM-driven agents can cryptographically sign their reasoning traces using lightweight hashes, ensuring an immutable audit trail of agentic decisions. By combining my expertise in MLOps and cryptographic validation, I intend to contribute to OpenAI’s mission of building safe, transparent, and verifiable AI systems that can operate securely across distributed, decentralized environments.
